Reading: Young Filmmakers in San Telmo

Hello! My name is Elena Paz, I am 17 years old, and I was born in Barracas, although I currently live in San Telmo, Buenos Aires. I love writing screenplays and taking street photographs, and I am very good at video editing. Next Thursday night, my film club and I are screening our first documentary at a historic theater on Defensa Street. We have filmed interviews with local artisans for seven months, and we have already edited the final cut.

Last year, our team didn't have professional microphones, so background street noise ruined many scenes. Therefore, we decided to record the narration inside a quiet library. While we were filming near Plaza Dorrego, a famous Argentine film director saw us and lent us his directional microphone. If the audience likes our documentary next Thursday, we will submit it to the BAFICI Independent Film Festival. However, our media teacher reminded us: "People won't connect with your film unless the audio is crystal clear."

Nowadays, our trailer is watched by thousands of film students online, and English subtitles are being translated by our classmates. In my view, documentary filmmaking is the most powerful art form I have ever explored, and it is much more authentic than commercial movies. Tomorrow afternoon, I am meeting the theater manager to test the projector. I am sure our premiere is going to be a huge success!
