Next Friday, my robotics team and I are traveling to Córdoba for the National Robotics Championship. If our robot completes the obstacle course in under two minutes, we will qualify for the international tournament. Still, our captain always warns us: "We won't win the championship unless we stay calm under pressure."

Tomorrow evening, I am meeting our sponsor to show him the final prototype. I am confident our team is going to bring the trophy back to Buenos Aires!
