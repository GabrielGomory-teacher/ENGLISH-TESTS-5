Reading: Rooftop Gardens in Boedo

Hello! My name is Camila Benítez, I am 16 years old, and I was born in San Cristóbal, although I now live in Boedo, Buenos Aires. I love gardening and cooking vegetarian dishes, and I am very good at composting organic waste. Next Saturday, my school group and I are hosting a workshop on urban agriculture for our neighbors. We have grown tomatoes, lettuce, and herbs on our school terrace since March, and we have already harvested fifty kilos of fresh vegetables.

Last year, our school terrace was completely empty and full of old desks. Therefore, we decided to build wooden planters using recycled pallets. While we were planting the first seeds, an agronomist from INTA walked by and gave us organic soil and irrigation pipes. If more neighbors start growing their own food, they will save money and eat healthier meals. However, our biology teacher reminded us: "Your plants won't survive the summer unless you water them every evening."

Nowadays, our fresh vegetables are used by the school kitchen every week, and natural fertilizers are being produced by our classmates. In my view, urban gardening is the most rewarding activity I have ever tried, and it is much cheaper than buying organic food at supermarkets. Tomorrow afternoon, I am meeting the school principal to ask for permission to expand our garden. I am sure our project is going to transform our neighborhood!
